Measuring Retrieval Quality

Recall at k reports the proportion of relevant passages that appear among the k
results returned. It answers whether the information reached the model at all.

Mean Reciprocal Rank, abbreviated MRR, averages one divided by the position of
the first correct result. Unlike recall, it rewards placing the right passage at
the top rather than merely including it somewhere in the list.

A golden dataset is a fixed collection of questions paired with the passages that
should answer them. Running the same questions after every change turns quality
into a regression test: if the numbers drop, the build fails.

Latency matters as much as accuracy in production. A pipeline that returns a
perfect answer after ten seconds is unusable for interactive search, so systems
are tuned against both dimensions at once.
